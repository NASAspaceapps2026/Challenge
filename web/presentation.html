What is on each slide

  1. Field Shift — when the soybean comes off and what should follow it: a decision-support tool for soybean farmers in Mato Grosso, Brazil.
  2. The stakes — Brazil’s second crop depends on a rain window that closes within weeks of the soybean harvest.
  3. The answer — Field Shift dates each field’s harvest (P10/P50/P90), then ranks what can follow.
  4. Four succession strategies — corn, corn + brachiaria, sorghum/millet and crotalaria, compared on income, water risk, soil cover and pest cycles.
  5. Built for the Brazilian farm — harvest delay, ENSO scenario, farm priorities, cattle, corn contract and known pests.
  6. El Niño years and cattle change the ranking — and the tool shows by how much.
  7. The evidence — 4.5-day mean harvest-prediction error, 26 seasons of NASA rainfall, 4 NASA datasets, open source.
